# Olist Brazil E-commerce Analysis

## About Olist Brazil E-commerce Company

Olist is a Brazil-based e-commerce platform that helps small and medium-sized businesses sell their products on major online marketplaces. Founded in 2015, it connects sellers to platforms like Amazon Brazil and Mercado Livre, allowing them to manage listings, orders, payments, and logistics from a single system.

Olist does not own inventory but acts as a facilitator between sellers and customers. It also provides tools for analytics, delivery management, and financial services, helping businesses scale efficiently in the digital marketplace.

The Olist dataset contains real transaction data including orders, customers, payments, and reviews—making it ideal for studying e-commerce performance and customer behaviour. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Business Problem Statement

An e-commerce company is experiencing inconsistent growth and lacks visibility into its end-to-end customer journey. Leadership aims to identify inefficiencies across the purchase funnel, understand regional and product-level performance, and improve customer satisfaction.

We have to find the answer of the below questions:

### Funnel Conversion Optimization
- What is the conversion rate from order creation → payment → delivery?
- At which stage does the highest drop-off occur?

### Revenue Leakage Analysis
- How much revenue is lost due to cancellations or failed deliveries?
- Which stages contribute the most to revenue loss?

### Regional Performance Deep Dive
- Which states generate the highest revenue?
- Which regions have low delivery success rates?

### Product & Category Intelligence
- Which product categories drive the most revenue?
- Which categories face high cancellations or low satisfaction?

### Delivery Performance & SLA Analysis
- What is the average delivery time?
- How do delivery delays impact customer satisfaction?

### Customer Experience & Reviews
- How do delivery delays correlate with review scores?
- Which customer segments are most dissatisfied?

### Customer Retention & Repeat Behaviour
- What percentage of customers are repeat buyers?
- Who are the high-value customers?

---

## Objective of the Analysis

The objective of this analysis is to identify bottlenecks in the e-commerce funnel, quantify revenue leakage, and evaluate factors influencing customer satisfaction and retention.

The insights generated will support data-driven decision-making to improve conversion rates, optimize delivery performance, and enhance overall business efficiency.
